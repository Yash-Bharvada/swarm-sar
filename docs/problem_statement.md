Problem Statement 5: Autonomous Disaster Response Swarm Robotics for Search and Rescue

Domain: AI for Robotics and Aerospace Engineering

Problem Statement: During catastrophic natural disasters like earthquakes or mine collapses, GPS-denied environments and unstable structures make human entry life-threatening. Teams are challenged to create a decentralized multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) framework that coordinates a heterogeneous swarm of autonomous drones and ground rovers. The robots must autonomously explore unknown, GPS-denied labyrinthine spaces, map survivors using thermal and acoustic sensors, share topological data peer-to-peer, and locate trapped victims efficiently.

Your Challenge: Design, train, and validate a robust prototype that successfully demonstrates end-to-end functionality, scalability, and real-world applicability within the hackathon timeframe.

Hints: Explore ROS2 (Robot Operating System) coupled with Gazebo or Isaac Sim for simulation. Use graph neural networks (GNNs) for inter-robot communication and collaborative SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping).
